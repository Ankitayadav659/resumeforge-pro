What recruiters at large companies look for

1. Relevance in 6 seconds

A headline that matches the job title, plus your most recent role and best skills where the eye lands first.

2. Impact, not duties

Bullets that start with action verbs and end with numbers: %, ₹/$, time saved, users, team size.

3. ATS-friendly format

One column, standard headings, no photos or tables, and keywords that mirror the job description.

4. One tailored copy per job

Duplicate your master resume and adjust the headline, summary and skills for each application.
