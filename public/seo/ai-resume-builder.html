Why an AI resume builder beats a blank template

Most resumes are rejected for the same few reasons: they list duties instead of results, they miss the keywords the applicant tracking system (ATS) is searching for, and they bury the most important information. A template fixes the layout, but it can't fix the writing. That is where AI helps.

ResumeForge Pro combines a live ATS and recruiter score with AI writing, so you see the problem and fix it in the same place. Every suggestion follows the standards used by recruiters at Fortune 500 companies, top Indian IT and product firms, consulting firms and banks.

Before and after: what the AI changes

Before: Responsible for managing social media pages and helping with campaigns.
After: Managed 4 social media channels and 12 campaigns, growing followers [X]x in [6] months.

The rewrite starts with an action verb, shows scope (4 channels, 12 campaigns) and leaves a clearly marked placeholder for the result. You add the real number, because recruiters verify claims in interviews, and an invented metric can cost you the offer.

Frequently asked questions

Is the AI resume builder free?

Yes. The free plan includes 3 resumes, every template, PDF download, the Recruiter Score and 3 AI actions a day. Pro raises the limits to 50 resumes and 60 AI actions a day.

Will the AI make up achievements or numbers?

No. The AI only uses facts already on your resume. Where a number would strengthen a bullet, it inserts a highlighted placeholder such as [X%] for you to fill in with the real figure.
