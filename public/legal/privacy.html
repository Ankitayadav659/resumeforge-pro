Privacy Policy

Your resume contains personal information, so we keep what we collect to a minimum and are clear about how it is used. We show no ads and never sell your data.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

1. Who we are

{{SITE}} (the "Service") at {{APP_URL}} is operated by {{BUSINESS}} ("we", "us", "our"). We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy. This policy is intended to meet our obligations under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and your password stored only as a secure hash (PBKDF2). We never store your password in readable form.
  • Resume content: everything you enter or upload into your resumes, including contact details, work history, education and skills, plus saved versions.
  • Job descriptions you paste for keyword matching or tailoring.
  • Cover letters you create or generate.
  • Usage and security logs: for example AI usage counts (to apply daily limits), share link view counts, and your IP address and request details, used for security and rate limiting.
  • Payment records: order ID, payment ID, amount, plan and status. We do not receive or store card numbers, UPI IDs or bank details.
  • Messages you send us through the contact form or by email.

Please do not put sensitive information that a resume does not need (such as government ID numbers, health details or bank details) into the Service.

3. Why we use it

  • To create and run your account and provide the features you use, such as editing, scoring, exporting and sharing resumes.
  • To run AI features when you ask for them.
  • To process payments and apply your Pro pass.
  • To send service emails, such as password reset links.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and enforce usage limits.
  • To answer your questions and support requests.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as keeping payment records for tax purposes.

We process your data based on your consent when you sign up and use features, and for legitimate uses permitted by law, such as security and legal compliance. We do not use your resume content for advertising, and we do not sell your personal data.

4. Who processes your data

We share data only with service providers that help us run the Service, and only what they need:

  • Hosting provider: stores our servers and database, where your account and resume data live.
  • Anthropic (AI provider): receives the relevant resume content, job description or uploaded resume text only when you click an AI feature or use AI import. Nothing is sent to Anthropic if you do not use AI features.
  • Razorpay (payments): processes your payment. You enter payment details directly with Razorpay, under Razorpay's own privacy policy.
  • Email provider (SMTP): delivers emails such as password resets.

We may also disclose data if required by law, court order or a government authority, or to protect the rights and safety of users and the Service.

5. Share links

Share links are off by default. If you turn one on, anyone with the link can view that resume until you turn it off. We count views so you can see them.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use a single essential cookie: a session cookie (HttpOnly) that keeps you logged in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your light or dark theme preference is stored in your browser's localStorage and is not sent to us.

7. How long we keep data

  • Your account, resumes, versions, cover letters and share links are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Free accounts keep the 10 most recent versions of a resume; Pro accounts keep 40.
  • Password reset tokens expire after 30 minutes.
  • Payment records may be kept for as long as tax and accounting laws require, even after you delete your account.
  • Security logs are kept only as long as needed for security and abuse prevention.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • Access and export your data at any time from Account settings.
  • Correct your data by editing your profile and resumes, or by contacting us.
  • Delete your account from Account settings, which deletes your resumes, versions and share links.
  • Withdraw consent by stopping use of a feature or deleting your account. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity, by writing to us.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 16 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We protect your data with measures including hashed passwords (PBKDF2), HttpOnly session cookies, rate limiting and access controls on our servers. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

11. International transfers

Some of our service providers, including our AI provider, may process data on servers outside India. Where data is transferred, we rely on providers who apply appropriate safeguards, and we follow any restrictions on transfers notified under Indian law.

12. Grievance officer and contact

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, contact our Grievance Officer at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} or through our contact page. Please use the email address registered to your account so we can verify your request. We aim to respond within 30 days.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify you by email or in the app.
