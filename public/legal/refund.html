Refund & Cancellation Policy

Pro passes are simple one-time purchases. This page explains when you can get a refund and how to ask for one.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

1. One-time passes, no auto-renewal

The Pro plan of {{SITE}} is sold by {{BUSINESS}} as a prepaid pass: 1 month (₹{{PRICE_1M}}), 3 months (₹{{PRICE_3M}}) or 12 months (₹{{PRICE_12M}}), paid once through Razorpay. Passes do not renew automatically and you are never charged again unless you choose to buy another pass. There is no subscription, so there is nothing to cancel. When your pass ends, your account simply returns to the Free plan and your resumes are kept.

2. When you can get a full refund

You can get a full refund if both of these are true:

  • you request it within 7 days of purchase, and
  • you have used fewer than 5 AI actions since that purchase.

AI actions cost us money each time they run, so this limit keeps the policy fair while still letting you try Pro.

3. Duplicate or failed payments

If you were charged twice for the same purchase, or your money was debited but the payment failed and Pro was not applied, we will refund the extra or failed amount in full, whatever the date or usage. Failed payments are often reversed automatically by your bank or Razorpay; if not, contact us.

4. When refunds are not available

Outside the cases above, including requests made after 7 days or after 5 or more AI actions have been used, passes are non-refundable, and we do not give partial refunds for unused time. If you have a problem with the Service, please contact us anyway; we will try to help.

5. How to request a refund

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} or use our contact page with:

  • the email address registered to your account, and
  • your Razorpay payment ID (shown in your payment confirmation from Razorpay).

We will confirm whether your request qualifies, usually within 2 business days.

6. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made through Razorpay to the original payment method (card, UPI, net banking or wallet). Once we process a refund, it usually reaches you within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

7. What happens to your account

When a pass is refunded, Pro access for that pass ends and your account returns to the Free plan. Your resumes stay in your account, and Free plan limits apply from then on.

8. Contact

Questions about payments or refunds? Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. See also our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
