Recruiter Review

From job duties to measurable impact

Hiring managers at large companies shortlist on results, not responsibilities. Every bullet gets checked for a strong opening verb, a number and a readable length.

  • Strong action verb at the start
  • A measurable result: %, ₹/$, time, users or team size
  • Under 30 words, so it fits in two lines
  • No "I / my", no clichés like "hardworking team player"
Before

Responsible for managing the company's social media pages and helping with campaigns.

  • Passive opener
  • No measurable result
↓
After

Managed 4 social media channels and 12 campaigns, growing followers 3.2x (12K → 38K) in 6 months.

  • Action verb
  • Scope + quantified outcome

Example for illustration. You supply the real numbers; the tool never invents metrics.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Will my resume pass ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems)?

All templates use one column, standard section headings and real text, with no tables, text boxes or images. These are the formats ATS parsers handle best. Paste the job description into the Recruiter Lens to check keyword coverage too.

I am a fresher with no work experience. Can I still build a strong resume?

Yes. Fresher mode puts Education, Skills and Projects first. It prompts for CGPA, internships, hackathons and leadership roles, and keeps the resume to one page, which is what campus and entry-level recruiters expect.

Is ResumeForge Pro free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 3 resumes, all templates, PDF download, the Recruiter Score and 3 AI actions a day. Pro passes add more resumes and AI, with a one-time payment and no auto-renewal.

Should I add a photo, date of birth or marital status?

Not for corporate or multinational roles. Large companies avoid these details to reduce bias, and they take up space. Your name, city, phone, email and LinkedIn are enough.

Where is my data stored?

On our secure servers. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Share links are off until you turn them on, and you can export or delete all your data at any time from Account settings.

One page or two?

Freshers and anyone with under 5 years of experience should aim for one page. Experienced professionals can use two. Executives may use up to three. The page counter and Recruiter Score warn you when you go over.
