Pricing questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan is free forever and includes 3 resumes, all 4 ATS-friendly templates, one-click PDF download, the Recruiter Score, job-description keyword matching and 3 AI actions a day.

Will I be charged automatically every month?

No. Pro is a prepaid pass for 1, 3 or 12 months. There is no auto-renewal. When your pass ends you move back to the Free plan and keep all your resumes.

Which payment methods are accepted?

Payments are processed securely by Razorpay. You can pay with UPI, debit and credit cards, netbanking and popular wallets. We never see or store your card or UPI details.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If Pro is not right for you, ask within 7 days of purchase and before using 5 AI actions, and we will refund you in full. See the refund policy for details.

What happens to my resumes if my Pro pass ends?

Nothing is deleted. You keep every resume and can still edit and download them. Creating new resumes beyond the Free limit, and higher AI limits, need an active Pro pass.
